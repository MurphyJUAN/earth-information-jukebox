Earth Information Jukebox

Listen to NASA Earth Information Center visualizations. Each pixel's color is read back through the visualization's own colorbar into a physical value — then that value is played as pitch.

Keyboard

  • 1 / 2 switch visualization
Visualizations: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio — Global Sea Surface Currents and Temperature (SVS 3912), Change in Night Lights 2012–2023, EIC version (SVS 5313). Sonification runs live in your browser (Web Audio API); nothing is pre-recorded.
